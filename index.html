科研项目：DF-SegDiff

Role: Researcher | Duration: Jan 2024 – Sep 2024 | Conference: IEEE-ICCD-2024

DF-SegDiff is a diffusion-based segmentation model for MRI brain tumor scans. The model improves segmentation accuracy while optimizing computational efficiency through distributed training and architectural improvements.

Key Contributions:

  • Data preprocessing: Cleaned MRI metadata and built feature matrices using Python (Pandas, NumPy).
  • Model development: Implemented DF-SegDiff in PyTorch with distributed fusion training, tested on BRATS2020 and MSD datasets.
  • Results: Improved segmentation accuracy and reduced training time.
